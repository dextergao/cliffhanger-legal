Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what the Cliffhanger app (“the app”) does with information about you. The app is published by the developer of Cliffhanger (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, write to support@thecherrytree.ai.

In short

The current version of the app does not collect personal data. What it records stays on your device, and it does not send information about you to us or to anyone else.

What the app keeps on your device

  • An identifier the app creates when it is installed, used only inside the app.
  • What you watch and where you stopped, so you can continue later.
  • The series you save to My List.
  • Your coins and Rewards progress.
  • Your settings and privacy choices.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and deleting the app removes it.

What leaves your device

Nothing about you. The videos and artwork are part of the app itself, so watching does not contact our servers. When you open a link in the app (for example this page, or our support email), your browser or mail app handles it under its own privacy terms.

Advertising and measurement

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising or measurement software. If a future version adds any, we will update this policy before that version is released, and on iPhone the app will ask your permission before any information is used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time to remove everything it stored. For any question or request about your information, write to support@thecherrytree.ai.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
