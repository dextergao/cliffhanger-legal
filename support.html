Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Write to support@thecherrytree.ai. Please include your iPhone model and the app version (shown at the bottom of the Me tab).

How do I unlock more episodes?

The first episodes of every series are free. Later episodes unlock with coins you earn in the Rewards tab.

Can I buy coins or a subscription?

Not in this version of the app.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Continue Watching and My List come back as long as the app stays installed.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app stores is on your device: deleting the app removes it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
